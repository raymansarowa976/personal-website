# Hello Readers

This is my first post, I plan to use this blog to write about stuff that 
interests me or just general stuff going on in my life.
First, I'll give a little backstory on who I am. My name is Rayman Sarowa
and I was born in the town of Penticton. My family came to Canada from India
and landed in the Okanagan valley. At first, we lived in a small town called Oliver,
which did not even have a hospital. This explains why I was born in Penticton.
I still have some family located in this town, but most of my extended and immediate family moved
to either the lower mainland or Kelowna. I've been in Kelowna since I was 5,
so most of my core memories were formed here. I did all my k-12 education here,
as well as my university. This is all I will write for now but I plan on telling more of my story 
in the future.
